KeepFit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

中文

KeepFit（管住嘴迈开腿）尊重并保护用户隐私。本政策说明我们如何收集、使用和存储信息。

1. 收集的信息

  • 你主动输入的数据：用户名、头像、身高、体重、目标体重、目标体脂、饮食记录、锻炼记录。
  • 可选同步数据：当你授权后，App 可读写 Apple 健康中的相关项目（如体重、锻炼、能量）。
  • 可选云同步数据：当你开启 iCloud 同步时，资料、设置以及相关记录会同步到你的 iCloud 账户。

2. 信息用途

  • 用于计算热量摄入、运动消耗、BMR/TDEE 及赤字趋势。
  • 用于展示图表、历史记录和个性化建议。
  • 用于在你授权时提供 Apple 健康与 iCloud 同步功能。

3. 存储与共享

  • 默认情况下，数据主要存储在你的设备本地。
  • 仅在你主动开启时，才会使用 iCloud 与 Apple 健康同步。
  • 我们不会将你的个人健康数据出售给第三方。

4. 权限说明

  • 照片与相机：用于头像和本餐照片的选择、拍摄。
  • 麦克风与语音识别：把饮食描述转为你可核对的文字。
  • 健康权限：用于 Apple 健康数据读写（可选）。
  • 通知权限：用于饮食/运动/每日总结提醒（可选）。

5. 云端饮食识别

启用此功能后，你同意并提交的本次饮食描述或照片会发往 KeepFit 服务器与 MiMo 模型服务，用于理解食物、本人份量和营养估算；后续补问会发送当前这餐的描述及结构化观察，不附带整份健康历史。

照片在发送前缩小、重新编码并移除拍摄元数据，KeepFit 不持久保存原图。语音优先在设备上转写；系统不支持时可能使用 Apple 语音服务。KeepFit 只接收你核对后的文字，不接收原始录音。

KeepFit 云端保存草稿、确认记录和 Apple 登录所需的去标识化标识。你可在“记一餐”右上角菜单删除云端识别数据，或通过删除 App 账号一并删除。当日用量计数会保留以防止绕过限额。模型服务方的数据处理规则请查看 MiMo 隐私政策。

6. 联系方式

如有隐私相关问题，请联系：keepfit@larryhu.com

English

KeepFit respects your privacy. This policy describes what data is processed and how it is used.

1. Data We Process

  • Data you enter: name, avatar, profile, goals, food logs, and workout logs.
  • Optional Apple Health data, only when you explicitly grant permission.
  • Optional iCloud synced data, only when you enable iCloud sync for profile, settings, and related records.

2. Purpose of Processing

  • Calorie, BMR/TDEE, and trend calculations.
  • Progress visualization and personalized recommendations.
  • Optional Apple Health and iCloud synchronization features.

3. Storage and Sharing

  • Data is primarily stored on-device by default.
  • Cloud/Health sync happens only if you enable and authorize it.
  • We do not sell personal health data.

4. Permissions

  • Photos and camera: avatar and meal photo selection or capture.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: editable transcription of meal descriptions.
  • HealthKit: read/write health metrics (optional).
  • Notifications: reminder scheduling (optional).

5. Cloud Meal Recognition

When enabled, the meal description or photo you consent to submit is sent to KeepFit and MiMo to understand foods, personal portions and nutrition estimates. Follow-ups include this meal's description and structured observations, without your full health history.

Photos are resized and re-encoded without capture metadata; KeepFit does not retain original images. Speech uses on-device recognition when supported, otherwise Apple speech services may process it. KeepFit receives the reviewed text, not raw audio.

KeepFit stores drafts, confirmed records and a pseudonymous identifier for Apple sign-in. Delete cloud recognition data from the Log a Meal menu or through app account deletion. Daily usage counters remain to prevent quota evasion. Provider terms are described in the MiMo privacy policy.

6. Contact

Privacy contact: keepfit@larryhu.com
